THE OBJECTIVE

Make the full ML workflow visible.

Student Performance Prediction demonstrates how a regression model can learn patterns from features such as study time, attendance, prior marks and assignment completion. It shows the estimate alongside the model's measured test performance.

The included dataset is synthetic and generated by a documented rule with random variation. It contains no real student records. Results are intended for coursework and demonstration, not academic decisions.

01Generate and inspect dataPreview, missing-value check, deduplication and cleaning.
02Prepare features safelyImputation, scaling and one-hot encoding inside a pipeline.
03Train and compareThree regressors share a reproducible held-out test split.
04Serve the estimateFlask validates entries and loads the saved best pipeline.
